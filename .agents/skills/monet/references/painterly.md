# Painterly profile

Use this profile for atmospheric, organic, impressionist, baroque, or figurative work.

Prioritize mood and light over literal description. Establish a chromatic underpainting, then let forms emerge through layered color, varied edges, and selective detail.

- Build surfaces from irregular translucent paths or dabs rather than one perfectly smooth fill.
- Add restrained `feTurbulence` grain when it makes the medium feel physical; keep it subordinate to the composition.
- For skin and other living forms, let a warmer saturated layer show through cooler local color at edges and transitions.
- Create lost edges with gradients or masks that merge shadows into the surrounding atmosphere.
- Use radial glows for luminous focal points and horizontal light bands for reflections on water.
- Clip energetic internal marks to a clean silhouette when the subject needs a firm outer contour.
- Vary blur by depth. Keep important edges and catchlights sharp enough to anchor the eye.
- Avoid plastic-looking stacks of flawless radial gradients, literal petal-by-petal construction, and uniform detail across the whole canvas.

Painterly SVG succeeds through controlled contrast between soft and hard, warm and cool, quiet fields and concentrated marks.
